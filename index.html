About Me

Bridging Africa
to the Decentralized Web

I'm Mahmoud Sardauna — a Web3 Content Strategist, Crypto Educator, and Aptos Contributor based in Nigeria. Since 2015, I've been at the intersection of blockchain technology, strategic communication, and community education.


As Lead at Bitkova Academy, I've built the premier multilingual crypto education platform in Northern Nigeria — reaching 15,000+ lives across Hausa-speaking communities. My work spans content strategy for Web3 brands, ecosystem growth for Aptos, and hands-on crypto trading expertise cultivated over a decade.


I believe financial freedom is a human right — and blockchain is the most powerful tool we have to deliver it to underserved populations. That's my mission, every day.
